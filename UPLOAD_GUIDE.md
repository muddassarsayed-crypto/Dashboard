## STEP 2 — Create Your Repository (Your Portfolio Folder)
⏱ Takes about 1 minute

A "repository" is just a project folder on GitHub. Think of it like a folder in Google Drive.

1. After logging in, click the **"+"** icon at the top right corner
2. Click **"New repository"**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Repository name:** `portfolio`
   - **Description:** `Data Analytics & Operations Portfolio — Sayed Muddassar`
   - **Visibility:** Select ✅ **Public** (IMPORTANT — must be public for the free website)
   - **Initialize:** Tick ✅ **"Add a README file"**
4. Click the green **"Create repository"** button

✅ Your portfolio folder is created!

## STEP 4 — Turn ON GitHub Pages (Make It a Live Website)
⏱ Takes about 2 minutes

This is the magic step that turns your files into a real website!

1. Click the **"Settings"** tab (at the top of your repository — it has a gear ⚙ icon)
2. In the LEFT sidebar, scroll down and click **"Pages"**
3. Under the heading **"Build and deployment"**, find **"Source"**
4. Click the dropdown that says **"None"** and change it to **"Deploy from a branch"**
5. Under **"Branch"**, click the dropdown that says **"None"** and select **"main"**
6. Leave the folder as **"/ (root)"**
7. Click **"Save"**
8. ⏳ Wait about 2 minutes...
9. Refresh the page — you will see a green box appear saying:
   **"Your site is live at https://sayed-muddassar.github.io/portfolio/"**

✅ Your portfolio website is now LIVE on the internet!

## ❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

**Q: The website is not showing after 2 minutes?**
A: Wait 5 minutes total and refresh again. Sometimes it takes a little longer the first time.

**Q: I see a 404 error?**
A: Make sure your file is named exactly `index.html` (all lowercase). GitHub is case-sensitive.

**Q: Can I update the files later?**
A: Yes! Just go back to your repository, click "Add file" → "Upload files" again, upload the new version, and commit. The website updates automatically within 1 minute.

**Q: Is this really free forever?**
A: Yes. GitHub Pages on public repositories is permanently free for personal portfolios.
